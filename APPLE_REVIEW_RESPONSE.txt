RESPONSES TO YOUR QUESTIONS
========================================

Question 1: Why does the app require phone number for registration?

Phone numbers are required for:

1. Expense Splitting with Contacts
   Users can add friends directly from contacts by phone number for quick expense splitting.

2. SMS Friend Invitations
   Users can invite friends who don't have the app via SMS. Phone numbers are stored in E.164 international format for accurate matching when invited users register.

3. Duplicate Account Prevention
   Phone numbers are validated to prevent users from creating multiple accounts.

4. User Identification
   Phone numbers serve as unique identifiers to help users find and connect with friends on the platform.

Technical:
- Phone numbers validated using libphonenumber-js library
- All normalized to E.164 international format
- Users can still use core features without providing contacts access

Question 2: Does your app upload user's contacts to our server?

SHORT ANSWER: Meet-n-Split DOES NOT upload or sync entire contact lists to our servers.

WHAT WE STORE:

1. User's Own Information (Registration):
   When a user registers, we store their email and phone number for:
   - User authentication and account management
   - Finding and connecting with other registered users
   - Sending friend requests and invitations

2. Selected Contacts for Invitations (User-Initiated Only):
   When a user explicitly selects specific contacts to invite, we store only those phone numbers to:
   - Check if those phone numbers are registered users
   - Create friend request records
   - Track pending SMS invitations
   - Match users when invited contacts later register

WHAT WE DO NOT DO:

- NO bulk contact upload
- NO background syncing
- NO contact details beyond phone numbers (no names, emails, addresses)
- Contacts only accessed when user taps "Add from Contacts"
- Users manually select which contacts to invite
- Users can revoke contacts permission anytime through device Settings
- App functions fully without contacts permission

DATA USAGE:
Stored phone numbers used exclusively for:
- Matching users for friend requests
- SMS-based invitation system
- Preventing duplicate accounts
- Connecting users who mutually invite each other

PRIVACY & SECURITY:
- Phone numbers stored in E.164 international format
- Secure storage in Firebase Firestore with proper access controls
- Users can delete account and all data anytime
- We comply with data protection regulations
