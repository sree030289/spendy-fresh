=== QUESTION 1: Why phone number required? ===

1. Expense Splitting: Add friends from contacts by phone for quick expense splitting
2. SMS Invitations: Invite friends via SMS, stored in E.164 format for matching
3. Duplicate Prevention: Validate phone numbers to prevent multiple accounts
4. User Identification: Unique identifier to find and connect with friends

Technical: Phone numbers validated using libphonenumber-js, normalized to E.164 format

=== QUESTION 2: Do you upload contacts? ===

NO - We DO NOT upload or sync entire contact lists.

What We Store:
1. User's own phone/email (registration)
2. Selected contact phone numbers ONLY when user explicitly chooses to invite them

What We DON'T Do:
✗ No bulk contact upload
✗ No background syncing
✗ No contact details beyond phone numbers
✗ Contacts only accessed when user taps "Add from Contacts"
✗ Users manually select which contacts to invite
✗ App works fully without contacts permission

Privacy: All data stored securely in Firebase with proper access controls. Users can delete account and all data anytime.
