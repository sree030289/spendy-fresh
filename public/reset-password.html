For your security, password resets must be completed in the Meet-n-Split mobile app using the OTP (One-Time Password) code sent to your email.

2
Tap "Forgot Password?" on the login screen
3
Enter your email address to receive an OTP code
4
Enter the 6-digit code from your email
5
Create your new password
📱 Open Meet-n-Split App
